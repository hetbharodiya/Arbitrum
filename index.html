Why Ethereum needed Layer 2

Ethereum is the world's most secure decentralized network, but its popularity led to network congestion, resulting in slow transaction times and prohibitively high gas fees. Layer 2 solutions act as a secondary framework built on top of the main chain to handle transactions off-chain, drastically improving throughput.

⛓️

What is Arbitrum?

Arbitrum is an Optimistic Rollup that batches multiple transactions together and submits them as a single proof to Ethereum. It assumes transactions are valid by default, achieving massive scale without sacrificing decentralization.

⚡

Real-World Benefits

By using Arbitrum over Mainnet, developers and users enjoy up to 10x lower gas fees and near-instant transaction confirmations. This makes DeFi protocols, NFT minting, and complex dApps economically viable for everyday use.

🛡️

Ethereum Security

Unlike sidechains or alternate Layer 1s, Arbitrum derives its security directly from Ethereum. It uses fraud proofs to resolve any disputes, ensuring that malicious actors cannot compromise the network.
